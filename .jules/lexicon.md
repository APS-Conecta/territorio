## 2026-10-04 - Retired datasets fallback label

When displaying recent import batches whose associated Dataset has been retired/removed, use the domain term `conjunto retirado` rather than `conjunto eliminado`. Under `CONTEXT.md`, the domain concept for deleted/removed records or datasets is **Retired** ("retirado"), and terms like "deleted" or "eliminado" must be avoided in user-facing prose and UI text.
